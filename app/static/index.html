How to read FACT

  1. Open the magazine: drag the bottom-right corner, click a corner, or press the right arrow key.
  2. Write the headline you want checked on the lines on page 2, then press Enter.
  3. If it came with a photo, click the dashed box or drop the image on it.
  4. Both verdicts print on page 3. Keep turning pages to see why.
